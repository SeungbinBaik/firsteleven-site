First Eleven Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 August 2026 · 한국어

Summary

First Eleven runs entirely on your device. It has no account, no server, and no analytics, and it sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.

Information we collect

None. First Eleven collects no personal information. There is no sign-up, no advertising identifier, no analytics SDK, no tracking technology, and no code that transmits data over the network.

Information stored on your device

  • Game saves — your career progress is stored on the device.
  • Player photo (optional) — a photo attached at career creation stays on the device and never leaves the app.
  • Settings — preferences such as sound, haptics and display currency are stored on the device.

iCloud backup

Game saves are mirrored as a backup copy to your personal iCloud Drive. That data lives only in storage attached to your Apple Account; the developer cannot access it. The game works fully without signing in to iCloud.

Notifications

Training-complete notifications are local notifications scheduled on the device. No push server is used.

Children's privacy

Because no user's personal information is collected, no child's personal information is collected either.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page. A change that begins collecting data would be announced explicitly alongside the app update.
